# Responsible Disclosure & Use Policy

## Purpose

ZenScan is a defensive endpoint auditing tool designed to help organizations inventory and audit their own assets for security best practices. It is **not** intended for unauthorized scanning or reconnaissance against third-party infrastructure.

## Authorized Use Only

Use ZenScan **only on**:
- Infrastructure you own or operate.
- Systems where you have explicit written permission to test.
- Internal networks and staging environments under your control.

## Prohibited Uses

Do not use ZenScan for:
- Scanning third-party systems without authorization.
- Bypassing network access controls or firewalls.
- Probing infrastructure belonging to other organizations.
- Automated recon against public services or search-engine scraping.

## Liability

This tool is provided as-is for defensive purposes. Users are solely responsible for ensuring compliance with applicable laws and organizational policies. The authors assume no liability for misuse or damages arising from this tool.

## Reporting Security Issues

If you discover a vulnerability in ZenScan itself, please email `security@example.com` with:
- A detailed description of the issue.
- Steps to reproduce.
- The version of ZenScan affected.

We will acknowledge your report within 7 days and work toward a fix.

## Questions?

If you have questions about authorized use, please open an issue on the repository or contact the maintainers.
